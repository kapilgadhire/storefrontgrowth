01 · About us

Strategy and execution, under one roof.

StorefrontGrowth is a full-service, AI-powered B2B marketing agency, not a design studio. We help B2B companies deliver growth-oriented marketing: demand generation, account-based marketing, content marketing, product launches, brand building and brand guidelines, website design and development, UI/UX design, events and exhibitions, PR and digital marketing.

That matters for a site the size of quantiphi.com. The people planning the information architecture are the same people designing the pages, writing the SEO framework and updating the brand guidelines, and they know what the site has to do for pipeline. Nothing is handed between agencies.

Why this matters for Quantiphi

Built for an AI company's buyers

Structure that helps enterprise buyers find solutions, products and industries quickly
Templates that let 400+ resource pages stay consistent as the library grows
SEO and AEO built into every design, so pages are ready for Google and AI search
Motion and micro-animations that support the story instead of slowing the page

04 · Timeline

From kickoff to handoff

Each phase ends with a review, so Quantiphi signs off before we move to the next tier. Total duration: 12 weeks.

Week 11 week

Discovery and information architecture

Stakeholder interviews, current-site and SEO audit, sitemap and navigation.

Weeks 2–43 weeks

Design strategy and concepts

Design directions, brand guideline updates and motion principles.

Week 51 week

L1 pages

Low- and high-fidelity designs for the 6 L1 templates, with motion for key heroes.

Weeks 6–72 weeks

L2 templates

The 7 L2 templates and the shared component library.

Weeks 8–103 weeks

L3 templates and SEO framework

The 6 L3 templates, SEO and AEO requirements, and asset production.

Weeks 11–122 weeks

Final review and handoff

Final files, brand guidelines and specs delivered to Quantiphi's team.
