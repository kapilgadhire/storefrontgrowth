01 · About us

Strategy and execution, under one roof.

StorefrontGrowth is a full-service, AI-powered B2B marketing team. We work with B2B, SaaS and AI companies on strategy, brand, websites, SEO/AEO, content and the analytics that tie them to pipeline.

For a site the size of quantiphi.com, that means the people planning the information architecture are the same people designing the pages, writing the SEO framework and updating the brand guidelines. Nothing is handed between agencies.

Why this matters for Quantiphi

Built for an AI company's buyers

Structure that helps enterprise buyers find solutions, products and industries quickly
Templates that let 400+ resource pages stay consistent as the library grows
SEO and AEO built into every design, so pages are ready for Google and AI search
Motion and micro-animations that support the story instead of slowing the page
